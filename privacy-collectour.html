Plain-language summary: CollectOur stores the collections you build — items, photos, locations, and notes — and shares them only with the people you invite. Face ID and fingerprint unlock happen on your device; we never receive biometric data. We don’t sell your data or use it for advertising.

1. About This Policy

This policy explains how CollectOur by Vortex Technologies Limited (“we”, “us”) handles personal data in its Android, iOS, and web apps. We are the controller of the data described here. This policy adds to the Vortex Technologies Privacy Policy; where the two differ, this policy applies to CollectOur.

2. Information We Collect

Your account

  • Email address and password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider).
  • Display name (initially taken from your email address; you can change it) and an optional profile photo.
  • Your plan (free or paid) and usage against plan limits.

Your collections

  • Collections you create or join, and your role in each (owner, admin, manager, or viewer).
  • Items and their details: for books, the ISBN, title, authors, genre, year, publisher, and cover; for equipment and assets, name, type, serial number, asset code, specifications, purchase price and date, and depreciation details.
  • Item condition, notes, and up to five photos per item.
  • Locations you define (such as rooms or shelves) and quantities. These are names you type, not GPS locations.
  • Maintenance tasks and logs, including who completed a task and when.
  • Ratings and comments members leave on items.
  • Invitations, including the email address of the person invited, even if they don’t have an account yet.

Collected automatically

  • Crash and error reports and sampled performance data (see Section 6).

3. Device Permissions and Biometrics

  • Camera — to scan book barcodes and asset QR codes, and to take photos of covers and items.
  • Photo library — to choose a profile picture, cover, or item photo. CollectOur only accesses images you select.
  • Face ID / fingerprint (optional) — to unlock the app instead of typing your password. This uses your device’s built-in biometric system. Your biometric data stays on your device and is never collected, stored, or sent to us; the app only records, on your device, whether you turned the feature on.

CollectOur does not use your location or contacts and does not send push notifications. It does not record audio.

4. How We Use Information

  • To store and show your collections and share them with the people you invite.
  • To look up book details from a scanned ISBN (see Section 6).
  • To apply plan limits.
  • To keep the app secure and working, and to fix crashes and errors.
  • To send account emails, such as sign-up and email-change confirmations.

Spreadsheet exports and printed QR labels are created on your device and are not sent to us.

5. Who Can See Your Information

  • Collection members can see everything in a shared collection — items, notes, photos, purchase prices, serial numbers, ratings, and who added what — along with each member’s display name and email address. Owners and admins can also see pending invitations. Think about who you invite before recording sensitive details.
  • Other CollectOur users can look up your display name, email address, and profile photo, which is how collection invitations find the right person.
  • Photos (profile photos, covers, and item photos) are stored at long, hard-to-guess web addresses. Anyone who has a photo’s address can view it without signing in, so don’t upload photos you need to keep strictly private.
  • Collections are not public and cannot be browsed by people who aren’t members.

7. Retention and Deletion

  • You can delete items, photos, and whole collections in the app. Deleting a collection deletes its items, members, invitations, and locations.
  • To delete your account, use the account deletion link on your Profile screen, which opens our Data Deletion Request page, or email info@vtechs.com.jm. We delete your account, profile, profile photo, and collections you own, including their photos. For items you added to other people’s collections, we remove your name from them or delete them if you ask.
  • Error reports are kept for a limited period, typically up to 90 days.

We complete deletion requests within 30 days of verifying them. Deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for a short period until they expire.

8. Children

CollectOur is not directed at children under 13, and children under 13 should not create accounts. Households, clubs, and classrooms can share collections, but each person with an account should be old enough to agree to this policy or have a parent’s permission. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact info@vtechs.com.jm and we will delete it.

9. Your Rights

You have the rights described in the Your Rights section of the Vortex Technologies Privacy Policy, including access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, and objection. You can export a book collection to a spreadsheet from the app at any time.

To exercise any of these rights for CollectOur, email info@vtechs.com.jm or use our Data Deletion Request page. We respond within 30 days and may ask you to verify your identity first. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Information Commissioner in Jamaica or the data protection authority where you live.

10. Changes and Contact

We update this policy when CollectOur changes the way it handles data — for example, when a feature, permission, or service provider is added or removed. The “Last Updated” date at the top shows when it last changed. For material changes we will also give notice in the app or by email where we can.

Questions about this policy: info@vtechs.com.jm.
