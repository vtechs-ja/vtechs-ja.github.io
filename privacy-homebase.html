Plain-language summary: HomeBase stores your household’s tasks, check-ins, and supplies so everyone in the home can see them. Children use profiles that a parent creates, with no email or login of their own. Household data is visible to the members of that household only. We don’t sell it or use it for advertising.

1. About This Policy

This policy explains how the HomeBase app (Android package com.vtechs.homebase) by Vortex Technologies Limited (“we”, “us”) handles personal data. We are the controller of the data described here. This policy adds to the Vortex Technologies Privacy Policy; where the two differ, this policy applies to HomeBase.

2. Information We Collect

Accounts and household setup

  • Full accounts: email address and password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider).
  • Household: household name, timezone, week start day, and a 6-character invite code.
  • Member profiles: display name, household role (parent, adult, teenager, or child), admin or manager permissions, and optional capability notes describing what a member can take on.
  • Profile-only members (typically children): display name, household role, and a 4-digit passcode set by a parent or admin. The passcode is stored only as a salted hash, and a profile locks after repeated wrong attempts.
  • Devices: a random identifier the app creates for each device (not a hardware identifier), whether the device is shared, and which profile last used it.

Household activity

  • Tasks, assignments, schedules, and completion history, including who completed or marked a task and when.
  • Weekly check-ins, including how the week felt, optional notes, and members an adult chose to recognise.
  • Reminder preferences for tasks.

Supplies and shopping

  • Household supply items, stock levels, shopping lists, shop runs, store names, prices paid, and who restocked what.
  • Product barcodes you scan. Product details found for a barcode are cached and shared across all HomeBase households; this cache contains product information only, not who scanned it.

Capability notes and check-in notes are free text. Please keep them to what your household needs, and avoid recording health information you don’t need to share.

Collected automatically

  • Crash and error reports and sampled performance data (see Section 6).

3. Device Permissions

  • Camera — to scan product barcodes and to photograph receipts when you choose “Log a receipt”. Photos are taken with the live camera; HomeBase does not access your photo library.

HomeBase does not request location, microphone, or contacts access. Push notifications are not currently enabled; if we turn them on, we will update this policy.

4. How We Use Information

  • To run your household’s shared view: tasks, check-ins, supplies, and shopping lists, updated in real time for household members.
  • To let children and other members use a shared device through passcode-protected profiles.
  • To read receipts you photograph and fill in items and prices for you (see Section 6).
  • To look up product details for barcodes you scan.
  • To keep the app secure and working, and to fix crashes and errors.
  • To send account emails such as password resets and email-change confirmations.

HomeBase does not score, rank, or profile members, does not show ads, and does not sell data.

5. Who Can See Your Information

  • Your household: everything in a household is visible to all members of that household, including member profiles, email addresses of full-account members, capability notes, task history, check-ins, and supplies. Admins and members with manage permission can change tasks and check-ins; any member can update supplies and shopping lists.
  • Joining: anyone with your household’s invite code can ask to join, so share it only with people in your home. An admin can pre-create a member with someone’s email address so they are matched when they join.
  • Nothing in HomeBase is public, and households cannot see each other’s data.

7. Retention and Deletion

  • Household data is kept while the household uses HomeBase so that shared history stays available.
  • Removing a member: an admin can remove a member in the app. Their active tasks are unassigned, but their past task history stays visible to the household.
  • Clearing data: admins can permanently clear all tasks or all supplies (and their history) from household settings.
  • Deleting an account or household: this is currently done on request. Use our Data Deletion Request page or email info@vtechs.com.jm. Deleting your account removes your login and profile details. Entries you made in a shared household, such as completed tasks and check-ins, may stay visible to the remaining members unless the whole household is deleted. To delete a household entirely, the admin should contact us.
  • Children’s profiles: a parent or household admin can remove a child’s profile in the app, or ask us to delete it completely.
  • Error reports are kept for a limited period, typically up to 90 days.

We complete deletion requests within 30 days of verifying them. Deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for a short period until they expire.

8. Children

HomeBase is designed for households that include children as active participants.

  • Children take part through profile-only members that a parent or household admin creates and manages. A child’s profile holds only a display name, household role, passcode hash, and the household activity linked to them. Children do not provide an email address and have no login of their own.
  • Full accounts are intended for adults and older teenagers who can sign up with their own email address. Children under 13 should not create full accounts.
  • Parents and admins can view, edit, lock, unlock, reset, and remove children’s profiles at any time.

If you believe a child has created a full account without a parent’s involvement, contact info@vtechs.com.jm and we will delete it.

9. Your Rights

You have the rights described in the Your Rights section of the Vortex Technologies Privacy Policy, including access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, and objection. A parent or household admin can exercise these rights on behalf of a child’s profile.

To exercise any of these rights for HomeBase, email info@vtechs.com.jm or use our Data Deletion Request page. We respond within 30 days and may ask you to verify your identity first. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Information Commissioner in Jamaica or the data protection authority where you live.

10. Changes and Contact

We update this policy when HomeBase changes the way it handles data — for example, when a feature, permission, or service provider is added or removed. The “Last Updated” date at the top shows when it last changed. For material changes we will also give notice in the app or by email where we can.

Questions about this policy: info@vtechs.com.jm.
