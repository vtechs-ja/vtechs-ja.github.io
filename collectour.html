What It Is

A catalogue for what's on your shelves

CollectOur is a native mobile app for Android and iOS that turns a pile of books into a searchable, shareable collection. Point your camera at a barcode and CollectOur looks up the title, authors, genre, publisher, and cover for you — you just review and save.

Collections can stay private, or be shared with other people as managers who help build the collection or viewers who can browse it. A household library, a book club's shelf, or a classroom's reading corner can all be kept in one place, by everyone who looks after it.

How It Works

From scan to shelf in seconds

Barcode Scanning

Fast, native camera scanning of ISBN barcodes, with automatic lookup of book details from public catalogues and a manual-entry fallback when a book can't be found.

Review Before Saving

Every scan lands on a confirm screen so you can correct the title, authors, or genre before it goes into your collection.

Duplicate Detection

Scan a book you already have and CollectOur asks whether it's another copy or a mistake — no accidental double entries.

Shared Collections

Invite other people as managers who can add and edit items, or viewers who can browse. Access is enforced on the server, not just in the app.

Collection Overview

See at a glance how many items you have, broken down by genre and published year, and how many distinct authors are on your shelves.

Filter, Sort & Export

Filter and sort any collection, and export it to an Excel spreadsheet straight from your phone.
