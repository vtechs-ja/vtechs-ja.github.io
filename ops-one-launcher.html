The Problem

You think in tasks. Your phone shows you a grid of icons.

Most home screens are an app drawer with a clock on top. When you want to call someone, get directions, or start a timer, you first have to remember which app does it and where you put it. Home screens that try to be smarter usually do it by sending your habits to the cloud. OpsOne takes a different approach. It starts from the action, works out the app for you, and does all of its learning on your phone.

What OpsOne Launcher Does

Tap what you want to do, not which app

Your phone is organised into areas — Now, People, Go, Play, Capture, Tools, and Device — built around the things you actually do.

CallMessageNavigatePlayPhotoTimer

Verbs with Smart Fallback

Each action resolves to the right app, shortcut, or system action, with a fallback chain if your first choice isn’t installed. Actions with nothing to handle them simply don’t appear.

Suggestions That Explain Themselves

OpsOne learns what you open at different times of day and on different days of the week. Every suggestion shows how confident it is and a “Why this?” explanation, and you can tell it “Not now” or “Don’t suggest”.

Your People, One Tap Away

Starred favourites and recent conversations from your messaging apps sit together. Pick a person, then call, text, or open WhatsApp.

One Search for Everything

Search replaces the app drawer. It finds apps, contacts, actions, and app shortcuts in one place, and apps sort themselves into the right area — including the banking apps Jamaicans use every day.

Now Playing, Front and Centre

Media controls for whatever’s playing sit on the Now screen, next to your suggestions and pinned actions.

Make It Yours

Switch between the Modern Blue and Retro Frame themes, each with night and driving variants, and set a matching lock screen in one tap. The layout adapts to phones and tablets.

Design Principles

What guides the product

01

Start from the Action

People think “call Mum”, not “open the dialer”. The home screen should meet you at the task and handle the plumbing.

02

Your Habits Stay on Your Phone

No account, no ads, no analytics. Everything it learns about you is stored on your phone, deleted automatically after 180 days, and can be cleared whenever you like.

03

Explain, Don’t Guess

Suggestions show their reasoning and stay put, so the screen never reshuffles under your thumb. If OpsOne isn’t sure, it doesn’t pretend to be.

04

A Home Screen Must Never Fail

If something goes wrong, a simple backup app list takes over, so you can always reach your apps.
