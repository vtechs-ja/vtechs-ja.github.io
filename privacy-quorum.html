Plain-language summary: Quorum helps club and association managers keep records of their members, attendance, dues, and equipment. Managers have accounts; members do not, and their records are entered and controlled by the group’s managers. We host the data for them, don’t sell it, and don’t use it for advertising.

1. About This Policy and Our Role

This policy explains how Quorum, a web app by Vortex Technologies Limited (“we”, “us”), handles personal data. It adds to the Vortex Technologies Privacy Policy; where the two differ, this policy applies to Quorum.

Quorum handles two kinds of personal data, and our role is different for each:

  • Manager accounts — the people who sign up and run a group in Quorum. We are the controller of this account data.
  • Member records — information that managers enter about the people in their group. Members do not have Quorum accounts. The club, association, or group is the controller of these records and decides what is recorded and why. We act as its processor: we store and process the records only to provide Quorum to the group.

If you are a member and want to see, correct, or remove your record, please contact your group’s managers first. If you can’t reach them, contact us at info@vtechs.com.jm and we will help.

2. Information We Collect

Manager accounts

  • Full name, email address, and password (the password is stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider).
  • Sign-in sessions, which may include your IP address and browser details.
  • The groups you create or manage, your manager invitations, and a record of which manager created or recorded each entry.

Member records entered by managers

  • Name, email address, phone number, date of birth, join date, status (active or inactive), and free-text notes.
  • Subgroups the member belongs to, which can include teams, cohorts, or households.
  • Attendance at events and training, including minutes attended.
  • Dues schedules and payment records (amount, date, method such as cash or transfer, and notes). Quorum only records payments made outside the app; no money passes through Quorum.
  • Equipment or resources lent to the member and their return dates.

Managers should record only what their group needs, and should avoid putting sensitive information (such as health details) in free-text notes.

Collected automatically

  • Server and hosting logs, including IP address, request details, and timestamps.
  • Error and performance reports (see Section 5).

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide Quorum: sign-in, storing and displaying your group’s records, and letting managers work together.
  • To keep the service secure and working, including investigating errors.
  • To contact managers about their account, such as password resets and important service notices.

We do not use member records for our own purposes, do not sell any data, and do not use Quorum data for advertising.

4. Who Can See Your Information

  • Within a group: every manager of a group can see and edit all of that group’s records, including other managers’ names and email addresses and pending invitations. Quorum does not currently have restricted manager roles.
  • Members cannot sign in and cannot see any records.
  • There are no public pages or public links to group data. Manager invitations are single-use links that expire after 7 days.

6. Retention and Deletion

  • Manager accounts are kept while the account is active. To delete your account, use our Data Deletion Request page or email info@vtechs.com.jm.
  • Groups and all their records can be deleted at the group’s request, made through our Data Deletion Request page. When a group is deleted, all its member, attendance, dues, and equipment records are deleted with it.
  • Member records: managers can mark a member inactive in the app. Inactive records are kept so the group’s history stays accurate. To permanently delete a member’s record, the group’s manager (or the member, if the group can’t be reached) can contact us.
  • Logs and error reports are kept for a limited period, typically up to 90 days.

We complete deletion requests within 30 days of verifying them. Deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for a short period until they expire.

7. Cookies

Quorum uses two essential cookies: a sign-in session cookie and a cookie that remembers which group you last opened (kept for up to one year). The app can be installed to your home screen; it caches only the app’s own files for offline use, not your group’s data. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies.

8. Children

Manager accounts are for adults. Quorum is used by sports clubs and community groups whose members may include children, so managers may enter records about minors (for example, a youth team). Children never sign in to Quorum.

Groups that record information about children are responsible for having the right to do so, including any parental consent their local law requires. If you are a parent and have a concern about your child’s record, contact the group or email us at info@vtechs.com.jm.

9. Your Rights

You have the rights described in the Your Rights section of the Vortex Technologies Privacy Policy, including access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, and objection. If you are a member of a group, we will usually pass your request to that group, since it controls your record, and help it respond.

To exercise any of these rights for Quorum, email info@vtechs.com.jm or use our Data Deletion Request page. We respond within 30 days and may ask you to verify your identity first. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Information Commissioner in Jamaica or the data protection authority where you live.

10. Changes and Contact

We update this policy when Quorum changes the way it handles data — for example, when a feature, permission, or service provider is added or removed. The “Last Updated” date at the top shows when it last changed. For material changes we will also give notice in the app or by email where we can.

Questions about this policy: info@vtechs.com.jm.
