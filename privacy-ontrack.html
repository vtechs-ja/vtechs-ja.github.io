Plain-language summary: OnTrack lets an association or club show members-only content on its WordPress site and companion app. Each organisation runs its own OnTrack site and decides who its members are; Vortex Technologies builds the software. OnTrack has no ads, analytics, or payments.

1. About This Policy and Who Is Responsible

This policy explains how OnTrack — a WordPress plugin and theme with a companion mobile app (package com.vtechs.ontrack), built by Vortex Technologies Limited (“we”, “us”) — handles personal data. It adds to the Vortex Technologies Privacy Policy.

Each organisation that uses OnTrack (for example, a church, association, or club) runs its own OnTrack website and appears under its own name in the app. That organisation is the controller of its members’ data: it creates member accounts, decides which groups and content each member can access, and is responsible for its own privacy notice. Vortex Technologies develops and supports the software and acts as the organisation’s processor when we host or maintain its site, handling member data only on its instructions.

If you are a member, contact your organisation first about your account or data. You can also reach us at info@vtechs.com.jm and we will help or pass your request on.

2. Information Processed

Member accounts

OnTrack does not have self sign-up; accounts are created by the organisation’s administrators. An account can hold:

  • Name, username, email address, and password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Date of birth, gender, city, and country, used by the organisation to organise groups and understand its membership.
  • Membership status, role (member, leader, community leader, or administrator), and group memberships.
  • Notes an administrator may add when reviewing whether a member’s details fit a group’s criteria.

Sign-in

  • Signing in to the app issues short-lived access tokens and a longer-lived refresh token. Only a hashed copy of the refresh token is kept on the server, and expired tokens are deleted daily.
  • To limit abuse, the server briefly keeps a hashed form of your IP address (for about one minute) when you stream audio.

Stored on your device by the app

  • Sign-in tokens, your display name, and membership status, in your device’s secure storage.
  • If you turn on biometric sign-in, your email address and password are saved in your device’s secure storage, protected by your fingerprint or Face ID. They stay on the device until you turn biometric sign-in off.
  • Downloaded audio, cached playlists, and app settings.

3. Device Permissions

  • Face ID / fingerprint (optional) — for quick sign-in, using your device’s built-in biometric system. Biometric data never leaves your device.
  • Background audio — so audio keeps playing when you leave the app.
  • Local network / casting — the app includes Google Cast support, which looks for Cast devices on your network.

The app does not use your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photos, and does not send push notifications.

4. How Information Is Used

  • To sign members in and show each member the content their membership level and groups allow — the same on the website and in the app.
  • To let leaders and administrators manage groups and view rosters and member details for the groups they look after.
  • To send password-reset emails.
  • To keep the service secure and working, and to fix crashes and errors.

5. Who Can See Your Information

  • Your organisation’s administrators, and leaders or coordinators for your groups, can see your profile, including demographic details, and your group memberships.
  • Other members cannot see your details. There are no public profiles or social features.

7. Retention and Deletion

  • Accounts are kept for as long as the organisation keeps you as a member. Deleting an account removes your profile and details from the organisation’s site.
  • To delete your account, ask your organisation’s administrators, or use our Data Deletion Request page and we will arrange it with them.
  • In the app, you can delete downloaded audio in Settings. Signing out removes your sign-in tokens; turning off biometric sign-in removes your saved email and password from the device.
  • Crash reports are kept for a limited period, typically up to 90 days.

8. Children

Organisations may create accounts for younger members. Where they do, the organisation is responsible for getting any parental consent its local law requires. Parents with questions about a child’s account should contact the organisation or info@vtechs.com.jm.

9. Your Rights

You have the rights described in the Your Rights section of the Vortex Technologies Privacy Policy, including access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, and objection. Because your organisation controls your account, we will usually pass your request to it and help it respond.

To exercise any of these rights for OnTrack, email info@vtechs.com.jm or use our Data Deletion Request page. We respond within 30 days and may ask you to verify your identity first. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Information Commissioner in Jamaica or the data protection authority where you live.

10. Changes and Contact

We update this policy when OnTrack changes the way it handles data — for example, when a feature, permission, or service provider is added or removed. The “Last Updated” date at the top shows when it last changed. For material changes we will also give notice in the app or by email where we can.

Questions about this policy: info@vtechs.com.jm.
