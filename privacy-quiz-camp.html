Plain-language summary: Quiz Camp stores your profile, quiz results, and progress so you can play solo or in live sessions hosted by your club’s coach. Club members never see ads. Solo players who haven’t bought Remove Ads see ads from Google AdMob. We don’t sell your data.

1. About This Policy

This policy explains how Quiz Camp (package com.vtechs.quizcamp) by Vortex Technologies Limited (“we”, “us”) handles personal data in its Android, iOS, and web apps. We are the controller of the data described here. This policy adds to the Vortex Technologies Privacy Policy; where the two differ, this policy applies to Quiz Camp.

2. Information We Collect

Your account

  • Email address and password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider). We send a code to your email to confirm it.
  • Display name, and optionally your country and city (or parish) and a profile picture.
  • Your XP, level, badges, streaks, and whether you have bought Remove Ads.

Playing and studying

  • Your answers, whether they were right, how quickly you buzzed and answered, and points scored.
  • Game results, per-category statistics, daily challenge completions, flashcard progress, study activity, bookmarks (with optional notes), and reports you make about questions.
  • Leaderboard entries (score and period, with your country and city if you added them).

Clubs and live sessions

  • Clubs you coach or belong to, your role (coach or member), and club XP and seasons.
  • Live sessions you host or join: your display name, team, role (quizmaster, player, or viewer), and scores.
  • Coaches: the questions, answers, notes, and photos, audio, or video you add to your question bank, and your club’s subscription status.

Collected automatically

  • Crash and error reports and sampled performance data (see Section 6).
  • For solo players who see ads: device and advertising information collected by Google AdMob (see Section 6).

3. Device Permissions

  • Camera — to scan QR join codes, and for coaches to take photos for questions.
  • Photo and video library — for coaches to add media to questions. Quiz Camp only accesses items you select.
  • Advertising ID (Android) — used by Google AdMob to serve ads to solo players, subject to your consent choices.

Quiz Camp does not use your location or contacts, does not record audio, and does not send push notifications.

4. How We Use Information

  • To run quizzes, live sessions, clubs, leaderboards, and your study progress.
  • To let coaches manage their clubs and question banks.
  • To show ads to solo players who haven’t bought Remove Ads, and to process in-app purchases.
  • To keep the app secure and working, and to fix crashes and errors.
  • To send account emails, such as sign-up codes and password resets.

5. Who Can See Your Information

  • Leaderboards show your display name and score, with your country and city if you added them. Choose a display name that doesn’t reveal your full name if you prefer.
  • Live sessions: other people in the session, and anyone watching through the session’s viewer link, can see participants’ display names, teams, and scores.
  • Clubs: coaches can see their members’ display names, join dates, and XP, and can remove members. Club members see the club leaderboard. Clubs are invite-only.
  • Other players can see the display name and profile picture of people they play with or compete against.
  • Quiz Camp has no chat, friends, or messaging features.

Advertising

  • Ads are shown only to signed-in solo players who are not in a club and haven’t bought Remove Ads. Club members never see ads, and the web app shows no ads.
  • When you first open the app, Google’s consent tool may ask whether you agree to personalised ads, depending on where you live. Full-screen ads between rounds are always non-personalised. Banner ads follow your consent choice.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings.

Club subscriptions: coach club plans are free during the pilot. When paid plans launch, payments will be processed by Stripe, and we will update this policy first.

7. Retention and Deletion

  • Your account, progress, and history are kept while your account is active.
  • To delete your account and data, use our Data Deletion Request page (choose Quiz Camp) or email info@vtechs.com.jm. We delete your profile, answers, progress, club memberships, and any question banks you own.
  • Live sessions older than 24 hours are closed automatically and their join codes are cleared.
  • Error reports are kept for a limited period, typically up to 90 days.

We complete deletion requests within 30 days of verifying them. Deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for a short period until they expire. Purchase records held by Google or Apple are governed by their policies.

8. Children and Young People

Quiz Camp is built for clubs, which may include school-age members who join through a coach’s invitation.

  • Children under 13 should use Quiz Camp only with a parent’s or guardian’s permission and through a club led by a coach they know. Club play is ad-free.
  • Coaches who invite young members are responsible for making sure their club has the parental permission required where they operate.
  • Young players should choose a display name that isn’t their full name and should not add their city.
  • Quiz Camp has no chat, messaging, or friend search.

If you are a parent and believe your child is using Quiz Camp without your permission, contact info@vtechs.com.jm and we will delete the account.

9. Your Rights

You have the rights described in the Your Rights section of the Vortex Technologies Privacy Policy, including access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, and objection. A parent or guardian can make requests on behalf of their child.

To exercise any of these rights for Quiz Camp, email info@vtechs.com.jm or use our Data Deletion Request page. We respond within 30 days and may ask you to verify your identity first. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Information Commissioner in Jamaica or the data protection authority where you live.

10. Changes and Contact

We update this policy when Quiz Camp changes the way it handles data — for example, when a feature, permission, or service provider is added or removed. The “Last Updated” date at the top shows when it last changed. For material changes we will also give notice in the app or by email where we can.

Questions about this policy: info@vtechs.com.jm.
