3. Our Role

Data protection law distinguishes between a controller, who decides why and how personal data is used, and a processor, who handles it on the controller’s behalf.

  • We are the controller for our website, for accounts people create in our products, and for products where we decide how data is used.
  • Some products let an organisation or professional keep records about other people — for example, a club’s member records or a practitioner’s client records. For those records, the organisation or professional is the controller and we are their processor. We handle that data only to provide the product to them, and we help them respond to requests from the people concerned.
  • When we build or maintain software for clients, we process their data only on their instructions and under our agreement with them.

Each product policy says which role applies.

4. Our Website

vtechs.com.jm is a static information site. It has no accounts, and it uses no cookies, analytics, advertising, or tracking.

  • Contact and data deletion forms do not send anything to our servers. When you submit one, the site opens your own email app with a pre-filled message to info@vtechs.com.jm, and nothing is sent until you press send. We then receive whatever the email contains (typically your name, email address, and message).
  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare. Like any web host, they process your IP address and browser details to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse, and may keep short-lived technical logs.
  • Fonts: pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when you visit.

5. Information in Our Products

Depending on the product, we may process:

  • Account information — such as your name, email address, and a password stored only as a secure hash.
  • Content you create — the records, items, photos, tasks, or other content that is the purpose of the product.
  • Records about other people that you or your organisation choose to keep in the product (see Section 3).
  • Technical data — such as device and app information, IP address, logs, and crash and error reports.
  • Purchase information — where a product offers purchases, a record of the transaction. Card details are handled by the payment provider or app store and are never stored by us.

Some products run entirely on your device and send us nothing. Each product policy lists exactly what that product collects and why.

6. How We Use Personal Data

  • To provide our products and services — accounts, sign-in, features, and support.
  • To keep them secure and reliable — preventing abuse and diagnosing crashes and errors.
  • To communicate with you — account emails such as verification and password reset, important service notices, and replies to your enquiries.
  • To meet legal obligations — including record-keeping and lawful requests from authorities.

We collect only what a product needs. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you. Where a product shows advertising, its product policy explains how.

7. Legal Basis for Processing

Under Jamaica’s Data Protection Act, 2020 and, where they apply, similar laws in other countries, we rely on:

  • Contract — to provide a product or service you have signed up for.
  • Legitimate interests — such as keeping our products secure and fixing errors, where these are not overridden by your rights.
  • Legal obligation — where the law requires us to process or keep data.
  • Consent — for optional features that depend on it, such as personalised advertising or optional crash reporting. You can withdraw consent at any time.

9. International Data Transfers

Vortex Technologies operates from Jamaica, but most of our service providers store and process data in the United States, and some in other countries. When personal data is transferred outside Jamaica, we use providers that protect it contractually to a standard consistent with the Data Protection Act, 2020, and we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses where required.

10. Data Retention

We keep personal data only as long as we need it for the purposes described here and in the product policies, unless the law requires us to keep it longer. As a general rule:

  • Account and product data is kept while your account is active and deleted when you or the controller ask us to delete it.
  • Crash reports and technical logs are kept for a limited period, typically up to 90 days.
  • Enquiries and support emails are kept for up to three years after the matter is closed.
  • Financial records of our own transactions are kept for at least seven years, as required for tax and accounting.

When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it. Deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for a short period until they expire.

11. How We Protect Your Data

We use technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including encryption in transit, access controls, and restricting access to people who need it. Our Security Policy describes our practices in more detail.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

12. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct data that is inaccurate or incomplete.
  • Delete your data, subject to legal retention obligations. You can use our Data Deletion Request page.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing.
  • Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on it.

To exercise these rights, email info@vtechs.com.jm. We respond within 30 days and may ask you to verify your identity first. Where another organisation controls your data (see Section 3), we will pass your request to it and help it respond.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Information Commissioner in Jamaica or to the data protection authority where you live.

13. Children

Our website is not directed at children. Some of our products are designed for households, clubs, or groups that include young people, and each of those products’ policies explains how children take part and what safeguards apply. We do not knowingly collect personal data from a child under 13 without appropriate parental involvement.

If you believe a child’s data has been collected without appropriate consent, contact info@vtechs.com.jm and we will delete it promptly.
