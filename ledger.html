What It Is

A running ledger, not a pile of invoices

Ledger works the way a well-kept paper ledger does. Each client's balance is simply the sessions they've been billed for, minus the payments they've made. There are no invoices to create, send, or chase — just a clear, always-current picture of who owes, who's settled, and who's in credit.

Scheduling and billing live in the same place, so marking a session as attended or a no-show updates the balance automatically. It's built to be plain, calm, and legible at a glance — nothing on screen competes with the numbers.

How It Works

Everything a solo practice needs to track

Clients

Keep contact details and notes for every client, with their balance shown right in the list and a full history of sessions and payments on their page.

Group Sessions

Any appointment can have one participant or many, each with their own rate and attendance status — ideal for group coaching and small classes.

Recurring Appointments

Set up weekly, fortnightly, or monthly series that end after a number of sessions or on a date, then adjust individual sessions without touching the rest.

Attendance & No-Shows

Mark each participant as attended, no-show, or cancelled. No-shows are billable, cancellations aren't — and the balance follows automatically.

Payments

Record payments against a client as they come in. They reduce the running balance without having to be matched to a specific session.

Dashboard

Total outstanding across all clients, your next upcoming sessions, and a list of who has a balance due — sorted by amount.

Who It's For

Practitioners who work for themselves

Coaches

Sports, fitness, music, and life coaches running a mix of one-on-one and group sessions.

Tutors

Independent tutors who need to track weekly lessons, missed sessions, and what each family owes.

Consultants

Solo consultants and freelancers billing by the session who want clean records without accounting software.
