Plain-language summary: Ledger is bookkeeping and scheduling for solo practitioners. Practitioners have accounts; their clients do not, and client records are entered and controlled by the practitioner. We host this data for them, keep it private to their workspace, don’t sell it, and don’t use it for advertising.

1. About This Policy and Our Role

This policy explains how Ledger, a web app by Vortex Technologies Limited (“we”, “us”), handles personal data. It adds to the Vortex Technologies Privacy Policy; where the two differ, this policy applies to Ledger.

  • Practitioner accounts — the coaches, tutors, consultants, and other practitioners who use Ledger, and anyone they invite to their workspace. We are the controller of this account data.
  • Client records — information practitioners enter about their own clients. Clients do not use Ledger. The practitioner is the controller of these records, and we act as their processor, handling the records only to provide Ledger to them.

If you are a practitioner’s client and have a question about your information, please contact the practitioner first. You can also reach us at info@vtechs.com.jm.

2. Information We Collect

Practitioner accounts

  • Email address and password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider), and optionally your name.
  • Sign-in sessions and one-time sign-in codes sent by email. Sessions may include IP address and browser details.
  • Your workspaces, their members and roles (owner or member), and invitations you send (the invitee’s email address).
  • Your plan (currently free for all users).

Records you keep in Ledger

  • Clients: name, phone number, email address, and free-text notes.
  • Appointments and sessions: dates, times, services, prices, durations, and attendance (scheduled, attended, no-show, or cancelled).
  • Payments received from clients (amount, date, note), and the resulting balances.
  • Expenses: category, date, amount, payee, and note.
  • Services and rates you offer.

Ledger records payments; it does not process them. It does not connect to bank accounts.

Collected automatically

  • Hosting logs, including IP address, request details, and timestamps.
  • Error and performance reports (see Section 5).

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide Ledger: sign-in, scheduling, bookkeeping, balances, and shared workspaces.
  • To keep the service secure and working, including investigating errors.
  • To send account emails, such as sign-in and verification codes.

We do not use client records for our own purposes, do not sell any data, and do not use Ledger data for advertising.

4. Who Can See Your Information

  • Within a workspace: every member of a workspace can see all of its clients, appointments, payments, and expenses, and the names and email addresses of the other members. Owners can invite and remove members. Access is enforced in the database, so one workspace cannot read another’s data.
  • Clients cannot see anything in Ledger, and there are no public pages or links.
  • Invitations are not emailed by Ledger. The invitee joins automatically when they sign in with the invited email address.

6. Retention and Deletion

  • You can delete individual clients, appointments, payments, expenses, and rates in the app at any time, or archive clients you no longer work with.
  • Your account and workspace are kept while your account is active. To delete your account and your workspace data, use our Data Deletion Request page or email info@vtechs.com.jm. If you share a workspace with others, tell us whether the workspace should be kept for them or deleted.
  • Logs and error reports are kept for a limited period, typically up to 90 days.

We complete deletion requests within 30 days of verifying them. Deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for a short period until they expire.

7. Cookies and Local Storage

Ledger uses essential cookies only: a sign-in session cookie and a cookie that remembers which workspace is active. Your light or dark theme choice is saved in your browser’s local storage. We do not use analytics or advertising cookies.

8. Children

Ledger accounts are for adults running a practice. Practitioners such as tutors may keep records about clients who are children; they are responsible for having the right to do so, including any parental consent their local law requires, and should keep notes about children to what they need.

9. Your Rights

You have the rights described in the Your Rights section of the Vortex Technologies Privacy Policy, including access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, and objection. If you are a practitioner’s client, we will usually pass your request to that practitioner, since they control your record, and help them respond.

To exercise any of these rights for Ledger, email info@vtechs.com.jm or use our Data Deletion Request page. We respond within 30 days and may ask you to verify your identity first. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Information Commissioner in Jamaica or the data protection authority where you live.

10. Changes and Contact

We update this policy when Ledger changes the way it handles data — for example, when a feature, permission, or service provider is added or removed. The “Last Updated” date at the top shows when it last changed. For material changes we will also give notice in the app or by email where we can.

Questions about this policy: info@vtechs.com.jm.
